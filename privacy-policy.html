Privacy Policy

Looks: AI Photo Editor

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Looks: AI Photo Editor app ("Looks", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

The short version: Your photos are edited entirely on your device. We do not upload your photos to any server, we do not require an account, and we do not sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

Photos and media. When you choose to take or select a photo, the app accesses that image only to let you edit it. All editing and AI filters run on your device — your photos are never uploaded to our servers or shared with us. Edited photos are saved only to your own device gallery when you tap Save.

Camera. Camera access is used solely to capture a photo you choose to edit. We do not record or transmit anything in the background.

Purchase information. Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play Billing and managed through RevenueCat. Through these services we receive your purchase and subscription status, a randomly generated app-user identifier, and basic technical information (such as device model, operating system version, app version, and country). We never receive or store your card number or full payment details.

What we do NOT collect. We do not collect your name, email address (unless you email us directly), contacts, precise location, or any copy of your photos.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide photo-editing and AI filter features.
  • To process subscriptions, unlock Pro features, and restore previous purchases.
  • To validate purchases and prevent fraud.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app.

3. App Permissions

  • Camera — to take a photo you want to edit.
  • Photos / Storage — to select a photo to edit and to save your edited image.
  • Internet — to process subscriptions and validate purchases.
  • Vibrate — for light haptic feedback in the interface.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Billing — payment processing. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

The app does not currently display third-party advertisements.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information only with the payment and subscription providers listed above, and only as needed to process your purchases.

6. Data Retention

Your photos remain on your device and are removed whenever you delete them from your device. Purchase records are retained by Google Play and RevenueCat as required to manage your subscription and for legal/accounting purposes.

7. Security

Because photo editing happens on your device and we do not host your images, your photos are not exposed to any transmission or server-side risk from us. Purchase data is handled over encrypted connections by Google and RevenueCat.

8. Children's Privacy

Looks is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will address it.

9. Your Rights

You can stop all data access at any time by revoking the app's permissions in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime through your Google Play account. For any request regarding your information, contact us at the email below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
